Sturdy’s Bittensor subnet, SN10, has allocated over $60m since its launch just a few short months ago. SN10 has demonstrated the potential for fully autonomous and permissionless yield optimization, with miners on the subnet decentralizing the role typically played by human asset managers. But to date, SN10 has been limited to optimizing aggregators within the Sturdy protocol.
