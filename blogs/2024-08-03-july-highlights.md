Sturdy had yet another busy month; from making the automated aggregation allocations more transparent with the Bittensor subnet dashboard, to using a grant from Aave to incentivize Sturdy’s new GHO aggregator, July has been a busy month! Here are all the major updates to the Sturdy ecosystem over the past 30 days: Bittensor Dashboard Sturdy’s Bittensor subnet just got even more transparent! The newly released dashboard enables users to track allocations, miner contributions, and better understand how the subnet informs aggregator allocations.
